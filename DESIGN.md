# Design System: NDBC Handyman Services

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Master Craftsman at Your Door"**

This design system replaces impersonal contractor templates with the reassuring moment a seasoned, 60-year-old master handyman knocks on a Northern Virginia front door holding a brass-clipped flat-rate estimate. Every surface pairs warm architectural stone and timber neutrals with authoritative slab-serif headings (`Bitter`) and civic-grade tabular typography (`Public Sans`).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Full-bleed doorstep portrait hero framed from the homeowner's perspective opening their front door.
- Zero eyebrows or kickers above headings—headings carry their own structural weight.
- Answer-first `.ai-summary` callouts and semantic comparison tables built for both homeowner clarity and 2026 AI Search Engine (GEO/AEO) citation.

## Colors

Grounded in Virginia cedar, forest green, warm limestone mortar, and brass hardware.

### Primary
- **Virginia Forest Green** (`#1b5e28`): Brand authority, primary links, verified checkmarks, and secondary action buttons.
- **Deep Pine** (`#11421b`): Hover states and high-contrast heading accents on light cards.

### Secondary
- **Burnished Amber Brass** (`#b45309`): High-intent conversion buttons (`Free Estimate`) and `:focus-visible` keyboard rings.

### Neutral
- **Warm Limestone** (`#f6f4ee`): Primary page ground.
- **Mortar Sand** (`#ece8dd`): Alternating section headers, table headers, and figure captions.
- **Timber Charcoal** (`#0f1912`): Top utility bar, hero scrim ground, and footer.

### Named Rules
**The Doorstep Contrast Rule.** Any text placed over photography sits over a directional timber-charcoal scrim (`rgba(11, 20, 14, 0.88–0.94)`) guaranteeing ≥7:1 contrast.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bitter (self-hosted WOFF2 variable 600–800, with Georgia fallback)
**Body Font:** Public Sans (self-hosted WOFF2 variable 400–700, with system sans fallback)

**Character:** Sturdy, craftsman slab-serif headings paired with a clean, high-trust sans-serif and `tabular-nums` for phone numbers, hours, and flat-rate specifications.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, `clamp(2.15rem, 4.4vw, 3.35rem)`, line-height 1.1): Hero `h1` thesis.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(1.65rem, 3vw, 2.35rem)`, line-height 1.16): Section `h2` headings.
- **Title** (700, `1.24rem`, line-height 1.2): Card and project `h3` headings.
- **Body** (400, `1.04rem`, line-height 1.65, max-width `68ch`): Primary narrative copy.

### Named Rules
**The No-Eyebrow Rule.** Never place small uppercase kicker pills or eyebrows above `h1` or `h2` headings; let the heading speak directly.

## Layout

1200px maximum container width with asymmetric 2-column hero and profile splits (`1.15fr / 0.85fr` and `0.95fr / 1.05fr`), 3-column project showcases, and 4-column sequential process steps. Collapses cleanly to a single column below `960px` with a sticky two-button mobile action bar (`Call` + `Free Estimate`).

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces declare elevation once—via a crisp `1px solid #d5cfc2` structural border at rest, never combining a `1px` border with a wide soft shadow ("ghost card").

## Shapes

Card radii use `12px` (`--radius`) and `16px` (`--radius-lg`) for feature figures; buttons and inputs use `8px` (`--radius-sm`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** `8px` radius, minimum height `46px`.
- **Primary / Accent:** Solid `#1b5e28` or `#b45309` with `#ffffff` text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** `12px` radius with `1px solid #d5cfc2` border and `#ffffff` background.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on phone numbers, hours, and specification tables.
- **Do** read all address and city strings from `site.js` (`site.primaryCity`, `site.primaryCityState`, `site.regionLabel`) so changing `activeAddressProfile` in `site.yaml` updates the entire site.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add kicker/eyebrow labels above headings.
- **Don't** add thick top or left accent borders on rounded cards.
